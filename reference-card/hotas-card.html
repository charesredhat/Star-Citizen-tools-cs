One action, one button

Two different actions on one input makes SC drop both — v_attack1 + v_attack2 on button 1 proved it, and it's why flight ready had to vacate 8 rather than share it. The rule is per device though, so camera view holds kb1_f4 and js1_button8 at once quite happily.

Dead axes — never bind these

js1_slider1 never moves at all, and js1_slider2 latches to maximum and stays there. The stick advertises six axes; only four are real. A binding on either of these is silently inert.

The throttle isn't called "throttle"

In 4.10's Master Modes the throttle rows are the longitudinal strafe family. v_throttle_abs does not exist and is discarded in silence. Increase is v_strafe_forward, decrease is v_strafe_back, and the lever axis is v_strafe_longitudinal.

Reading actionmaps.xml

It records only what differs from SC defaults. After loading, entries like v_pitch and v_attack1 will be missing — that means they match the default and are live, not that they failed.
